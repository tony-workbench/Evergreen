# Image commands (Gemini, `tools/gen.py`)
Each block is the shell command that produced the final file of that name. `gen.py OUT.png "prompt" [reference images...]`; shell variables such as `$K` or `$L` are defined at the top of the same command. Edits change one thing per call and list what must stay identical; the 2K size (`IMAGE_SIZE=2K`) is what made small objects (the phone) controllable. `E2_coffee`, `E3_yawn` and `E4_asleep` were made earlier with the same method from `E_ots`; their commands were not kept.

## A_woman

```bash
mkdir -p img10/rejected
L="Handmade needle-felted wool and clay stop-motion miniature, visible fibres, soft even studio light. No text, no labels."
(ASPECT=16:9 python3 gen.py img10/A_woman.png "Character sheet of ONE needle-felted woman puppet on a plain mid-grey seamless background. Nothing else in the picture: no furniture, no bags, no bottles, no slippers, no plants. Image 1 shows the puppet's general look in its middle panel (the standing figure with a ponytail); ignore the other panels of image 1 completely. Draw five views of the same puppet in a row, all standing on the same floor line: four full-figure views at exactly the same scale, standing upright with arms relaxed: front view, right side view, back view, three-quarter front view; and at the far right one head-and-shoulders close-up. She has black wool hair in a high ponytail tied with an orange band, a pale cream felt face, two small plain black bead eyes, NO eyebrows, NO eyelids, a small simple nose, a small calm closed mouth, soft pink cheeks, a grey short-sleeved t-shirt, dark navy shorts with a thin white side stripe, bare felt legs, and plain white ankle socks, no shoes. Her expression is neutral and calm. $L" sheet_woman.png &
ASPECT=16:9 python3 gen.py img10/B_tree.png "Image 1 is a character sheet of a small felt pine tree puppet with four views. Recreate the same sheet on the same plain background with FIVE views at exactly the same scale in one row: keep the first four views exactly as they are (front, side, front waving, running), and add a fifth view at the far right: the same puppet seen from directly BEHIND, a plain cream-white felt back in three scalloped tiers with the thin green stitched outline, no face at all, thin green wire arms hanging at its sides, a short brown felt trunk, and orange sneakers seen from behind. cd img10 && ffmpeg -v error -y -i A_woman.png -i B_tree.png -filter_complex "[0]scale=1300:-2[a];[1]scale=1300:-2[b];[a][b]vstack" AB.jpg
```
