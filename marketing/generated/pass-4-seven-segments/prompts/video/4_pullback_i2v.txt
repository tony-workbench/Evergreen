Handmade stop-motion miniature, needle-felted wool and clay, shallow depth of field. Real-time motion; the picture never freezes. One continuous shot: no cuts, no dissolves, no fades. No text, no speech.

MOTION between the two images: the camera dollies straight back and a little lower at one constant smooth speed for all 5 seconds, no zoom, and arrives exactly on the last image, the wide view of the room.

The desk stays one short desk; the paper stack, shut laptop, cup and phone do not move or turn.

In the first half second the tree presses the laptop lid the last bit down until it is shut flat and its light is out; the phone's glow fades.
Then the tree, in contact with a surface the whole time except one short drop: walks to the right edge of the desk, sits on the edge, slides off, lands on both feet beside her chair, and looks up at her. It never floats and never rises above the desk.
She stays seated facing the desk and follows it with her eyes and a small tilt of the head, at most 30 degrees.

AUDIO: quiet room tone, tiny footsteps, one soft thump, the same soft piano.
