A 25-second handmade stop-motion short film: wool-felt and modelling-clay puppets on a miniature set, slightly stepped puppet motion, shallow depth of field. Eight shots joined by hard cuts, in this exact order. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos.

Characters, identical in every shot:
- The woman is the felt puppet from [Image10]: black wool hair in a ponytail with an orange tie, bead eyes, grey running t-shirt, dark shorts, white running shoes.
- The tree is the small pine tree puppet from [Image9]: cream-white felt in three scalloped tiers with a thin green stitched outline, bead eyes, pink cheeks, wire arms, orange sneakers. It is knee-high to her and is never green.

The set: one small room. Desk with a laptop under the window on the left, a felt week board on the wall with seven columns headed S M T W T F S, the door on the right. The board changes only as described.

Shots 1 to 6 are one rainy evening in cold blue light. Shots 7 and 8 are the next morning in warm golden sunlight. The light changes only at the cut from shot 6 to shot 7.

Shot 1 (0-3 s). Starts as [Image1]. Medium close-up at her eye level. She stands at the door with her hand on the handle, dressed to run, and smiles. Behind her the week board holds one green running-shoe badge in the third column. She starts to turn the handle. Camera still.

Shot 2 (3-5.5 s). Starts as [Image2]. Low angle looking up at the week board. Grey felt badges with briefcase, video-camera and envelope icons fly in and slap onto the board one after another. The green running-shoe badge is knocked off and tumbles down toward the camera. Fast, a small shake on each hit.

Shot 3 (5.5-9 s). Starts as [Image3]. High wide shot of the room. She has let go of the door and sits down at the desk, shoulders dropping. A stream of grey badges flies out of the laptop screen in an arc and lands on the board, filling its first three columns. The green badge lies on the floor under the board. Camera slowly pushes in.

Shot 4 (9-12 s). Starts as [Image4]. Medium close-up at desk height. She folds forward and rests her forehead on her arms beside the laptop. Her phone lies dark next to her elbow. In the background the first three columns of the board are full of grey badges and the green badge is on the floor. Rain runs down the window. Camera still.

Shot 5 (12-15.5 s). Starts as [Image5]. Macro shot on the desk at the tree's eye height. The phone lights up soft green and the tree climbs out of its screen, pulls itself onto the desk and looks at her kindly. Behind it she lifts her head and stares. Slow push in on the tree.

Shot 6 (15.5-18.5 s). Starts as [Image6]. Close-up on the week board. The first three columns are full of grey badges. The tree hangs from the top edge of the board and presses the green running-shoe badge onto the empty fourth column. The badge begins to glow warm gold and the glow grows until it fills the frame.

Shot 7 (18.5-22 s). Starts as [Image7]. Next morning, warm sunlight, same room, low medium shot. She is back at the laptop. The tree drags her white running shoes across the floor by their laces to her chair and tugs at her. She looks down, laughs and closes the laptop. The green badge glows in the fourth column of the board.

Shot 8 (22-25 s). Starts as [Image8]. Medium shot at the open door, sunrise outside on a wet street. The tree runs out through the doorway first, pulling her by one finger. She follows it out, laughing, and they run off down the street together to the right. Camera follows them to the doorway and tilts up to the bright sky.

Sound: rain on glass and soft felt thuds in shots 1 to 6, a small chime when the badge glows, birdsong and light footsteps in shots 7 and 8. No speech, no music.
