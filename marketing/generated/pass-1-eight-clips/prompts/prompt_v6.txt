A 25-second handmade stop-motion short film. Everything is wool felt and modelling clay on a miniature set: visible fibres, slightly stepped puppet motion, shallow depth of field. Eight shots in this exact order, joined by hard cuts. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no speech.

THE WOMAN is the felt puppet in [Image10]: black wool hair in a ponytail with an orange tie, bead eyes, grey running t-shirt, dark shorts. She looks the same in every shot.

THE TREE is the small pine tree puppet in [Image9]: cream-white felt in three scalloped tiers with a thin green stitched outline, bead eyes, pink cheeks, thin wire arms, orange sneakers. It is knee-high to her. It is never green and never changes size.

THE ROOM: a desk with a laptop under the window on the left, a felt week board on the wall with seven columns headed S M T W T F S, a door on the right. The week board is background. It is only ever in soft focus except in shot 2.

LIGHT: shots 1 to 6 are one rainy evening, cold blue. Shots 7 and 8 are the next morning, warm gold. The light changes only at the cut from shot 6 to shot 7, never inside a shot.

Shot 1 (0-3 s). First frame is [Image1]. Medium close-up at her eye level, camera still. She stands at the door with her hand on the handle, dressed to run, with a small hopeful smile. Behind her, out of focus, the week board has one green running-shoe badge in its third column. Action: she breathes in, smiles a little more and begins to turn the handle. The shot ends before the door opens.

Shot 2 (3-5.5 s). First frame is [Image2]. Low angle looking steeply up at the week board. Action in order: three grey felt badges with briefcase, video-camera and envelope icons fly in from the left one after another and slap onto the board, each hit with a small camera shake; on the third hit the green running-shoe badge is knocked loose and tumbles down toward the camera until it fills the frame and goes dark. No characters in this shot.

Shot 3 (5.5-9 s). First frame is [Image3]. High wide shot of the whole room, camera slowly pushing in. She is sitting at the desk in front of the laptop. Action: her shoulders drop; a stream of grey badges keeps flying out of the laptop screen in an arc across the room and landing on the left columns of the week board. The green running-shoe badge lies still on the floor below the board and nobody touches it.

Shot 4 (9-12 s). First frame is [Image4]. Medium close-up at desk height, camera still. Action: she lowers her forehead onto her crossed arms beside the laptop and stays there, breathing slowly. Her phone lies dark on the desk next to her elbow. Rain runs down the window. In the soft-focus background the left three columns of the week board are full of grey badges and the green badge is on the floor.

Shot 5 (12-15.5 s). First frame is [Image5]. Macro shot on the desk at the tree's eye height, slow push in on the tree. Action in order: the phone screen lights up soft green; the tree climbs out of the screen, pulls its legs up onto the desk and stands; it looks up at her kindly. Behind it, out of focus, she lifts her head from her arms and stares at it. The green glow of the phone is the only colour.

Shot 6 (15.5-18.5 s). First frame is [Image6]. Extreme close-up on the tree; the tree is the subject and fills most of the frame. The week board behind it stays completely out of focus. Action in order: the tree, on tiptoe, strains and pushes the green running-shoe badge flat against the board with one wire arm; the badge sticks; a warm golden glow spreads from the badge over the tree's face; the tree opens its eyes and smiles; the glow grows until the whole frame is warm gold.

Shot 7 (18.5-22 s). First frame is [Image7]. The next morning, same room, warm sunlight through the window, low medium shot, camera still. She sits at the laptop again, typing. Action in order: the tree drags her pair of white running shoes across the floor by their laces to her chair; it tugs once at her sock; she looks down, laughs, and closes the laptop with one hand. In the soft-focus background the green badge glows in the fourth column of the week board.

Shot 8 (22-25 s). First frame is [Image8]. Outside her front door just after rain, low golden sun, wet shining street. The tree is in front and leads; she wears the white running shoes. Action in order: the tree runs to the right along the street pulling her by one finger; she runs after it, laughing; they run away from the camera down the street together; the camera tilts up from them to the bright open sky and holds on the sky.

SOUND: shots 1 to 6, steady rain on glass and soft felt thuds when badges land, one small chime when the green badge sticks in shot 6. Shots 7 and 8, birdsong, light footsteps, her short laugh without words. No music.
