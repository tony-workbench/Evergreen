# Problems found in pass 3

These are what the pass 4 images and prompts were written to fix.

## Push-in (0-4 s)
- The phone slides across the desk, because the wide start image and the close end frame show it in different places.

## Desk (4-19 s)
- While she is slumped over her arms, one hand keeps tapping her forearm for no reason.

## Laptop closing (19-25 s)
- The laptop closes faster than the two seconds written.

## Pull-back (25-30 s)
- The tree floats off the desk: about half a second in the air, arms out.
- Her head is turned a full 90 degrees while her body still faces the desk. The end image asked for exactly that. A person turns the chair first and then looks.
- The phone rotates with nothing touching it, because it lies at a different angle in the start frame and the end image.

## Shoes (30-48 s)
- The running shoes were drawn too big from the first storyboard on: each as long as the tree is tall, while her foot is about half that. The model shrinks them when she puts them on.
- She never pulls out the second lace; the shoe is just "tied" after two seconds.
- The segment starts about 10% tighter than the previous one ends.

## Door (48-59 s)
- When she stands, the oversized shoes stay on the floor and she walks out in other footwear.
- The tree is waist-high next to her at the door instead of knee-high.
- They walk out holding hands instead of the tree leading.
