One continuous shot, no cuts, no dissolves, no fades. Handmade stop-motion miniature: needle-felted wool and clay, shallow depth of field. No text, no speech.

The video starts exactly on the first image: a wide view of a dim cold blue room; a felt woman sits at a desk under a sunset window with her back to the camera, head propped on her left hand, typing slowly with one finger of her right hand.

CAMERA: one single move. The camera dollies straight forward toward her back at one constant, smooth speed for the whole 4 seconds, with no stutter, no skipped frames, no jump and no zoom, travelling across the room until it arrives just behind her right shoulder, exactly on the last image. The door and the running shoes slide out of frame on the right as the camera advances.

ACTION: during the move she keeps typing with one finger, slower and slower; in the last second her hand stops, her head leaves her propping hand and droops forward toward her arms on the desk, eyes closing, so that she arrives in the pose of the last image.

The desk is ONE short desk and stays one piece for the whole move: it never splits, stretches or grows a second table, and no second cup or second phone ever appears. The single coffee cup and the single phone stay in the same places on the desk, close beside the laptop at her right arm, from the first frame to the last. Nothing on the desk appears, disappears or changes: one stack of paper on the left, the laptop with a document on screen and three yellow sticky notes, one coffee cup with a white lid and one phone on the right.

AUDIO: quiet room tone, slow soft one-finger typing that stops in the last second. No music. Nothing loud.
