One 30-second handmade stop-motion short film, made as a single piece. Everything is needle-felted wool and modelling clay on a miniature set: visible fibres, slightly stepped puppet motion, shallow depth of field. The whole film is one evening in real time, filmed as ONE UNBROKEN TAKE by one real camera: no cuts, no time jumps. No subtitles, no logos, no speech.

THE TREE is the small pine tree puppet in [Image1]: cream-white felt in three scalloped tiers with a thin green stitched outline, bead eyes, pink cheeks, thin wire arms, orange sneakers. It is small: its top reaches only to the woman's knee. Its body is always cream-white, never green.

THE WOMAN is the puppet in [Image2]: black wool ponytail with an orange tie, pale cream felt face, small black bead eyes, no eyebrows, grey t-shirt, dark shorts, white socks. Her face stays this simple; tiredness is shown by her body, never by dark circles.

THE ROOM is [Image3], and nothing in it moves unless stated. A wooden desk stands against the far wall under a wooden window with an orange sunset. The woman sits at the desk on a wooden chair with her back to the room, facing her laptop; the laptop faces her. On the desk: left of the laptop one tall stack of paper; right of the laptop, by her right hand, one paper coffee cup with a white lid and a phone lying screen-up. Three yellow sticky notes with scribbled handwriting are on the laptop: two on the lower corners of the screen frame, one on the palm rest. A closed wooden door is in the right-hand wall, and a pair of white running shoes stands on the floor beside it. The room is dim and cold blue; the only warm colour is the sunset. The close view of the desk is [Image4].

PACE: unhurried. Every action finishes before the next one starts. She moves like someone fighting sleep: heavy, slow, a beat late.

THE CAMERA is one small physical film camera at about her shoulder height, on a dolly: it only rolls, turns and rises or sinks a little; it never jumps. Its path: it starts at the back of the room behind her; rolls forward until it is just behind her right shoulder; stays there; later sinks and turns right to follow the tree across the floor to the door and back; then turns to the door and rolls slowly toward it as the two of them run out; the camera itself never leaves the room.

0-3 s. The take starts on [Image3]. The camera rolls slowly forward toward her back. She is slumped, head propped on her left hand, typing with one finger of her right hand.

3-6 s. The camera arrives behind her right shoulder, as in [Image4]: we see her from behind, the lit screen with a document on it, and the things on the desk. Her head sinks slowly forward, then jerks back up; she shakes her head once.

6-9 s. With her RIGHT hand she picks up the coffee cup from the right of the laptop, tips her head back and drinks; the cup is empty; she lowers it and puts it back in exactly the same place. Her left hand stays on the keyboard.

9-12 s. She yawns widely, head tilting back, and rubs her right eye with her right fist, the same hand that held the cup. Her left hand stays on the keyboard.

12-15 s. Her head nods forward again, and this time it keeps going: she folds her arms on the desk in front of the keyboard and her head comes down to rest on them, face turned to the right, eyes closed. Then nothing moves for one full second.

15-18 s. The phone on the desk lights up soft green. The pointed top of the tree rises out of the screen; the tree pulls itself out and stands on the desk beside the phone, as in [Image5]. It looks up at the sleeping woman.

18-20.5 s. The tree walks to the right side of the laptop, reaches up with both wire arms, and presses the screen down on its hinge until the laptop is completely shut; the pale light goes out. At the click the woman lifts her head from her arms and stares at the tree with a small round mouth: surprised, not smiling.

20.5-23.5 s. The tree hops off the desk onto the floor. The camera sinks and turns right to follow it: it runs to the door, takes the laces of the white running shoes over its shoulder and drags the pair back across the floor toward her. Meanwhile she swivels a quarter turn to her right on the seat, her whole body turning together, until she sits sideways with both legs out over the right side of the chair and her socked feet on the floor, as in [Image6]. The chair itself does not move.

23.5-26 s. The tree drops the laces at her feet and points one wire arm at the door. She looks at the shoes, then at the tree, and for the first time in the film she smiles, then laughs silently. She pulls on both shoes and ties the laces.

26-30 s. She stands. The tree runs to the door and pushes it open; warm orange sunset light spills across the floor and over both of them. Holding her by one finger, the tree runs out through the doorway and she runs after it, as in [Image7]. The camera stays INSIDE the room and only rolls slowly forward toward the open doorway: framed by the dark door frame and the cold blue walls, the two of them take their first running strides out into the warm orange street. They do not run far away. The film ends on the open door with the two of them running in the warm light beyond it.

SOUND: one continuous soundtrack with no break. A single soft felt-piano melody from the first second to the last: slow, sparse and low until she falls asleep; a few brighter notes when the tree appears; warmer from the moment the laptop shuts; opening up as the door opens; ending on one long final note as they run out. Under it: slow one-finger typing, the hollow tap of an empty paper cup set down, a yawn-like breath, a soft thud as her head lands, the click of the laptop closing, laces dragging on wood, the door creaking, then light running footsteps and evening birdsong. No voices.
