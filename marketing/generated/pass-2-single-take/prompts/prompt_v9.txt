One 25-second handmade stop-motion short film, made as a single piece. Everything is wool felt and modelling clay on a miniature set: visible fibres, slightly stepped puppet motion, shallow depth of field. The whole film is one evening in real time: no time jumps, no flashbacks. Two scenes and exactly one cut, at 18 s. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no speech.

THE TREE is the small pine tree puppet in [Image1]: cream-white felt in three scalloped tiers with a thin green stitched outline, bead eyes, pink cheeks, thin wire arms, orange sneakers. It is knee-high to the woman. Its body is always cream-white, never green, and it never changes size.

THE WOMAN is the felt puppet in [Image2]: black wool hair in a ponytail with an orange tie, small black bead eyes, grey t-shirt, dark shorts, white socks, no shoes on.

THE ROOM is [Image3]: a small room at dusk. A wooden desk with an open laptop and a stack of papers stands under the window on the left; her phone lies on the desk to the right of the laptop. A wooden door is on the right, and a pair of white running shoes stands on the floor beside the door. Through the window the sky is orange with sunset. Inside, the room is dim and cold blue, lit mostly by the laptop screen. The walls are bare.

SCENE 1 (0-18 s), inside the room. ONE UNBROKEN TAKE with no cuts. The camera moves smoothly and always follows whoever is acting. The room stays dim and cold blue for the whole scene; the only warm colour is the sunset outside the window.

0-2.5 s. The take starts on [Image3]: a wide view of the whole room, camera slowly pushing in. She sits at the desk with hunched shoulders and types fast with both hands. Her face is tired and flat. She does not smile.

2.5-5 s. Her hands stop. She turns her whole head to the right, away from the laptop, until she is looking straight at the white running shoes by the door, and she holds that look for one full second with her mouth slightly open. Then the laptop screen flashes brighter with a new message. She flinches, turns her head back to the screen, breathes out, and her shoulders sink lower. She puts her hands back on the keys.

5-8 s. She runs out of strength, in four clear steps: first her typing slows to one finger; then her hands stop and she rubs her eyes with one hand; then her head tips forward; then her forehead comes down onto her crossed arms on the desk and her ponytail flops over. She stays completely still. The camera has pushed in to a close view at desk height: her head on her arms, the laptop, and the dark phone beside her elbow. She never smiles in the first 8 seconds.

8-10.5 s. The camera moves in on the phone until the phone is large in the frame. The phone screen lights up soft green. The pointed top of the tree rises out of the screen; the tree puts both wire hands on the edge of the phone, pulls itself all the way out and stands up on the desk. The woman has not moved; she is still face down.

10.5-13 s. The camera stays close on the tree; the tree is the subject and fills about half the height of the frame. The tree looks at the sleeping woman, then at the glowing laptop. It walks to the laptop, reaches up, puts both wire hands on the top edge of the laptop lid and pushes the lid down until the laptop is fully shut. The blue light of the screen goes out. At the sound, the woman lifts her head from her arms and stares at the tree with wide eyes and a small round mouth: surprised, not smiling yet.

13-15.5 s. The camera follows the tree down: it hops off the desk onto the floor, runs across the floorboards to the door, grabs the laces of the white running shoes and drags both shoes back across the floor to her chair. Low camera at floor height, close on the tree.

15.5-18 s. The camera rises to show both of them. The tree drops the laces at her feet and points one wire arm at the door. She looks down at the shoes, then at the tree, and now, for the first time in the film, she smiles, and the smile grows into a silent laugh. She pushes her chair back and stands up.

CUT at 18 s to SCENE 2 (18-25 s), outside her front door, as in [Image4]: the same evening, a few moments later. Low orange sunset sun, warm light on a quiet street of small felt houses and round felt trees. She now wears the white running shoes.

18-21 s. Side view at the open front door. The tree runs out first, pulling her along by one finger. She runs after it, laughing, ponytail swinging.

21-23 s. The camera turns to follow them from behind as they run away down the street side by side, getting smaller.

23-25 s. The camera tilts up from them to the wide orange sky and holds completely still on the sky.

SOUND: one continuous soundtrack for the whole film that never restarts at the cut. A single soft melody on felt piano runs from the first second to the last: slow, sparse and low from 0 to 10 s; a few brighter notes when the tree appears; warmer and fuller from the moment the laptop shuts; open and happy outside. Under the melody: fast keyboard tapping that slows and stops, one short message ping at 4 s, a soft thud when her head lands, a soft clap when the laptop shuts, laces dragging on wood, then light running footsteps and evening birdsong. No voices.
