One 25-second handmade stop-motion short film, made as a single piece. Everything is wool felt and modelling clay on a miniature set: visible fibres, slightly stepped puppet motion, shallow depth of field. The whole film is one evening in real time: no time jumps, no flashbacks. The whole film is ONE UNBROKEN TAKE filmed by one real camera: no cuts at all. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no speech.

THE TREE is the small pine tree puppet in [Image1]: cream-white felt in three scalloped tiers with a thin green stitched outline, bead eyes, pink cheeks, thin wire arms, orange sneakers. It is knee-high to the woman. Its body is always cream-white, never green, and it never changes size.

THE WOMAN is the felt puppet in [Image2]: black wool hair in a ponytail with an orange tie, small black bead eyes, grey t-shirt, dark shorts, white socks, no shoes on.

THE ROOM is [Image3]: a small room at dusk. A wooden desk with an open laptop and a stack of papers stands under the window on the left; her phone lies on the desk to the right of the laptop. A wooden door is on the right, and a pair of white running shoes stands on the floor beside the door. Through the window the sky is orange with sunset. Inside, the room is dim and cold blue, lit mostly by the laptop screen. The walls are bare. Outside the door is the street in [Image4].

THE CAMERA is one small physical film camera on a dolly, about knee height to the woman, with a real lens: shallow depth of field, slight parallax as it moves, objects close to the lens pass by out of focus. It never jumps, never teleports and never changes lens. It only ever does four things: roll forward, roll back, turn, and rise or sink a little. Its path through the film: it starts just inside the door, next to the running shoes, looking across the room at the desk; it rolls forward to the desk; it sinks to desk height; it follows the tree back across the floor to the door; it follows the two of them out through the doorway onto the street; it stops on the street and tilts up to the sky.

INSIDE (0-18 s). The room stays dim and cold blue the whole time; the only warm colour is the sunset outside the window.

0-2.5 s. The take starts on [Image3]. The camera is just inside the door: the white running shoes are large and soft-focus in the near foreground at the edge of the frame, and across the room she sits at the desk with hunched shoulders, typing fast with both hands. The camera rolls slowly forward toward her. Her face is tired and flat. She does not smile.

2.5-5 s. Her hands stop. She turns her whole head toward the camera and the door until she is looking straight at the running shoes, and she holds that look for one full second with her mouth slightly open. Then the laptop screen flashes brighter with a new message. She flinches, turns her head back to the screen, breathes out, and her shoulders sink lower. She puts her hands back on the keys. The camera keeps rolling forward; the shoes slide out of the frame.

5-8 s. She runs out of strength, in four clear steps: first her typing slows to one finger; then her hands stop and she rubs her eyes with one hand; then her head tips forward; then her forehead comes down onto her crossed arms on the desk and her ponytail flops over. She stays completely still. The camera arrives at the desk and sinks to desk height: her head on her arms, the laptop, and the dark phone beside her elbow. She never smiles in the first 8 seconds.

8-10.5 s. The camera rolls the last short distance toward the phone until the phone is large in the frame. The phone screen lights up soft green. The pointed top of the tree rises out of the screen; the tree puts both wire hands on the edge of the phone, pulls itself all the way out and stands up on the desk. The woman has not moved; she is still face down, out of focus behind it.

10.5-13 s. The camera holds close on the tree and turns to keep it in frame; the tree fills about half the height of the frame. The tree looks at the sleeping woman, then at the glowing laptop. It walks to the laptop, reaches up, puts both wire hands on the top edge of the lid and pushes the lid down until the laptop is fully shut. The blue light of the screen goes out. At the sound, the woman lifts her head and stares at the tree with wide eyes and a small round mouth: surprised, not smiling yet.

13-15.5 s. The tree hops off the desk onto the floor. The camera sinks to floor height and rolls back the way it came, staying close in front of the tree as the tree runs across the floorboards toward the door, grabs the laces of the white running shoes and drags both shoes back to her chair.

15.5-18 s. The camera rises a little to hold both of them. The tree drops the laces at her feet and points one wire arm at the door. She looks down at the shoes, then at the tree, and now, for the first time in the film, she smiles, and the smile grows into a silent laugh. She slips her feet into the shoes and stands up.

THROUGH THE DOOR (18-21 s). No cut. The camera turns to face the door from inside the room. The tree runs to the door and pushes it open; a strip of warm orange sunset light falls across the floor and widens as the door swings. The tree runs out pulling her by one finger, and she follows. The camera rolls forward right behind them, through the doorway: the door frame passes close by both edges of the lens, out of focus, and the cold blue room gives way to the warm orange street. The light changes only because the camera has moved from inside to outside.

OUTSIDE (21-25 s). The street is [Image4]: low orange sunset sun, small felt houses, round felt trees. The camera rolls a short way out onto the street and stops.

21-23 s. Seen from behind, they run away down the street side by side, she laughing with her ponytail swinging, getting smaller.

23-25 s. The camera, standing still on the street, tilts up from them to the wide orange sky and holds completely still on the sky.

SOUND: one continuous soundtrack for the whole film with no break. A single soft melody on felt piano runs from the first second to the last: slow, sparse and low from 0 to 10 s; a few brighter notes when the tree appears; warmer and fuller from the moment the laptop shuts; opening up as the door opens, happy outside. Under the melody: fast keyboard tapping that slows and stops, one short message ping at 4 s, a soft thud when her head lands, a soft clap when the laptop shuts, laces dragging on wood, the door creaking open, then light running footsteps and evening birdsong. No voices.
