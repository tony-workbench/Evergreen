One 25-second handmade stop-motion short film, made as a single piece. Everything is wool felt and modelling clay on a miniature set: visible fibres, slightly stepped puppet motion, shallow depth of field. Three scenes with only two cuts. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no speech.

THE TREE is the small pine tree puppet in [Image1]: cream-white felt in three scalloped tiers with a thin green stitched outline, bead eyes, pink cheeks, thin wire arms, orange sneakers. It is knee-high to the woman. It is never green and never changes size.

THE WOMAN is the felt puppet in [Image2]: black wool hair in a ponytail with an orange tie, small black bead eyes, grey t-shirt, dark shorts. She looks the same in every scene.

THE ROOM is the room in [Image3]: a desk with a laptop under the window on the left, a felt week board on the wall with seven columns headed S M T W T F S, a door on the right with a pair of white running shoes beside it. On the board the first three columns each hold three grey work badges; the other columns are empty. A round green running-shoe badge lies on the floor below the board.

SCENE 1 (0-15 s): one rainy evening, cold blue light. ONE UNBROKEN TAKE, no cuts; the camera moves smoothly and always follows the action.
- 0-3 s. The take starts on [Image3], a wide view of the room. She types at the laptop. She turns her head and looks at the running shoes by the door, then back at the screen.
- 3-5 s. The camera pushes in toward the desk. Her shoulders drop and she lowers her forehead onto her crossed arms. Her phone lies dark next to her elbow.
- 5-8 s. The camera keeps pushing in and sinks to desk height, close on the phone. The phone screen lights up soft green and the tree climbs out of the screen and stands on the desk. She lifts her head and stares at it, tired and puzzled. The tree waves at her.
- 8-11 s. The camera follows the tree as it hops from the desk down to the floor, runs to the green running-shoe badge, and lifts it over its head with both wire arms.
- 11-15 s. The camera stays close on the tree; the tree fills most of the frame and the board is soft-focus background. The tree carries the badge to the board, stretches up on tiptoe and presses it onto the empty fourth column. The badge sticks and glows warm gold. The tree smiles. The gold glow grows until it fills the frame.

CUT, through the gold glow, to SCENE 2 (15-20 s): the next morning in the same room, as in [Image4]. Warm sunlight through the window, no rain. Low medium shot, camera almost still. She sits at the laptop, typing. The green badge glows in the fourth column of the board. The tree drags her white running shoes across the floor by their laces to her chair and tugs at her sock. She looks down, laughs silently, and closes the laptop with one hand.

CUT to SCENE 3 (20-25 s): outside her front door, as in [Image5]. Low golden sun, wet shining street after rain. She wears the white running shoes. The tree runs ahead and pulls her by one finger; she runs after it, laughing. They run away from the camera down the street. The camera tilts up from them to the bright open sky and holds on the sky for the last two seconds.

SOUND: one continuous soundtrack for the whole film, never restarting at a cut. A single soft melody on felt piano runs from the first second to the last: slow, sparse and low in scene 1, opening up and brighter from the moment the badge glows, and warm and full in scenes 2 and 3. Under the melody: steady rain on glass in scene 1, one small chime when the badge sticks, then birdsong and light footsteps. No voices.
